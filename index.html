01 · Focus Areas

Where neural signals meet
the genome.

My work sits at the point where neural computation meets clinical urgency. I build frameworks and pipelines that turn raw biological signal — from EEG waveforms to genomic variants — into decisions researchers and clinicians can act on.

On the neuroscience side, I design privacy-first BCI frameworks with real-time neural signal processing for wearable EEG systems. On the genomics side, my variant triage pipelines model p53 suppressor mutations — combining folding dynamics with structural context to separate passengers from drivers.

The through-line is simple: tools that shorten the distance between data and discovery.
